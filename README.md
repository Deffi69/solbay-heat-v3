SOLBAY HEAT is an original open-world crime game set in Solbay, a sun-soaked coastal city of nine-by-nine streets with a downtown, a beach boardwalk pier, a marina, a harbour warehouse district and an airfield. Cross it on foot or by car — any car is yours to steal — and run five story missions (First Wheels, Borrowed Shine, Corsair Static, Hot Cargo, Solbay Goodbye) with dialogue, a stolen gold coupe, a pier shootout, a timed cargo run and a four-star escape to the runway, while the Solbay PD answers with a five-star wanted system, routed pursuit AI, ramming and gunfire. Side jobs, combat, a live minimap, a full HUD, synthesised audio and a localStorage save round out the city. Everything — the city, the characters, the vehicles, the signs and the synth notes — was invented for this game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move/drive, mouse to look (click the view to lock it), **E** to enter or steal a car and to talk, click to shoot, **F** to punch, **R** to reload, **Shift** to sprint/boost, **Space** to jump/handbrake, **C** camera, **P** pause, **M** mute.
